Slingshot Warrior Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · applies to the Slingshot Warrior app for Android · replaces the policy of 29 October 2026

Slingshot Warrior keeps your progress on your phone. If you go online, the game also keeps your board name, scores and a backup of your progress on our game server, without asking who you are. It has optional video ads (Google AdMob), optional purchases (Google Play) and error reports (Sentry). We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and you can delete your online data at any time.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file on your phone. It holds things like:

  • your scores and the board name you choose;
  • the stages you have reached, your saved runs and your settings;
  • the Eureka coins, slingshots, looks, songs, stickers and Validations you have earned or bought, and your Warrior Pass progress;
  • a record of purchases already paid out, so none is paid twice.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes this file.

Online leaderboards, pools and cloud save

When the game goes online, it signs in to our game server (run on Supabase, a database service) anonymously: the server gives your copy of the game a random ID, with no email, phone number or Google account attached. The server keeps:

  • the board name you choose (anyone can see it on the online boards; offensive names are refused);
  • your best scores on each board, with the loadout and stage of the run that set them;
  • for the Ratio League, the season totals of your ranked Arcade runs (number of runs, shots, hits, best streaks and best multipliers);
  • the friend pools you create or join, and their codes;
  • a backup copy of your game progress (the file above), so you can move it to a new phone with a one-time code;
  • if you tap Find a Rival or Auto-fill League, your place in the waiting list and the rival or league pool you are matched into. Only players who tap these are matched; the matching uses your best score.

This data is sent encrypted and is used only to run the boards, pools and cloud save. We do not sell it or use it for ads.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → Board name & cloud save → Delete my online data, then tap again to confirm. This removes your board name, online scores, Ratio League totals, pool memberships, cloud backup and your anonymous sign-in from our server straight away. Your progress on the phone stays.
  • Without the game (for example if it is uninstalled): email us at the developer address on Slingshot Warrior's Google Play page with your board name and, if you can, a pool code or the date you last played. We will delete the matching data within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Error reports (below) are deleted automatically by Sentry after its retention period (90 days at most).

Error reports (Sentry)

If the game hits an error or crashes, it sends a report to Sentry, an error-tracking service, so we can fix it. A report contains the error, the game's recent log, your phone model and Android version, the game version, and a short summary of your progress (for example coins, equipped slingshot and pass tier). It does not include your name, email, contacts or your IP address. If you use Report a problem in Settings, your note is sent the same way. Sentry stores reports in the European Union.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example about a rival or the Warrior Pass) when you leave it. They are made on the phone from your own game data; nothing is sent to us to do this, and the game does not look at anything else on your phone. It sends at most one a day, around the time you usually play, and never late at night. Turn them off in Settings → Board name & cloud save → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video ad, for example for a revive or a few Eureka coins. Ads only play when you tap to watch one. They are served by Google AdMob, which collects and shares some data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

  • your phone's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • how you interact with the ads (for example views and taps);
  • diagnostic information such as load times and errors.

This data is sent encrypted. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is covered by how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy Eureka coin packs, the Lucky Packet and the Warrior Pass. Payments are made through Google Play, which handles your payment details under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never see your card or account details. The game only receives a confirmation that an item was bought, so it can deliver it. No paid item is random.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-up, and no email address, phone number or other contact details asked for in the game.
  • No selling of your data, and no use of your game data for advertising.
  • No access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete their online data.

Changes to this policy

If a later version of the game changes what it collects, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any question about this policy, or to ask us to delete your data, contact us using the developer email address shown on Slingshot Warrior's Google Play page.
